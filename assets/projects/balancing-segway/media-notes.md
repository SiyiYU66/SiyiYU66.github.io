# Balancing Segway media

- `balancing.mp4`: web copy of Siyi Yu's supplied `balancing.mp4`, encoded at 720 × 720 with the original duration and audio stream. The source remains unchanged.
- `poster.webp`: frame at 4 seconds from the supplied video, also used as the disc cover. Adjust `coverCrop` in `dist/segway-study.js` to replace or reposition it.
- `pybench.webp`: reference image supplied by the user, from https://ruaridhmurdoch.com/projects/segway/pybench.webp . It shows the PyBench platform, not a claim that its hardware was designed by Siyi Yu.
- `control-loop.svg`: explanatory diagram based on the user's project description. It depicts the control architecture; it is not an experimental result plot.

The separate surface maps in `assets/disc-surfaces/balancing-segway/` are generated from the stable project ID and do not alter the colour artwork.
